Game Studio

5 ideas, you pick one. The Level Bot lists the proposed idea and levels, the Questioner and Betterment bots sharpen it (with a before vs new comparison), then it is designed, coded, tested and improved until it scores 8/10. Every bot shows its verdict.
Missing a file? The Helper goes Wizard, Designer, Coder, Reviewer, Tester, then adds it to the current game.
